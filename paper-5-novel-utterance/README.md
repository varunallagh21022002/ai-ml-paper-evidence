## Status
This notebook and its saved outputs were executed previously. It adapts the paper with Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct, Reuters Word2Vec, MiniLM, and TF-IDF on the authors’ public evaluation workbooks. It is not an exact reproduction and reports mixed evidence. `results.csv` and `pr_curves.png` are preserved here as the saved artifacts.

## Reproduction
Run all cells in Colab. The notebook installs its dependencies, downloads the public workbooks and Reuters corpus, and regenerates its outputs.

## Limits
Different LLM, embedding corpus, data path, and compute from the paper; no claim about the authors’ results.
